Setup Xenon

Xenon needs a couple of macOS permissions to see your screen and control the mouse and keyboard. Click Open Settings for each item, grant access, then click Check Again.

♿
Accessibility
Required to click, type, and press keyboard shortcuts. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and toggle on Xenon.

Screen Recording
Required to take screenshots so Xenon can see what is on screen. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording and toggle on Xenon.
